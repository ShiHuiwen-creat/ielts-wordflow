## Hometown
### Do you plan to continue living there?
I plan to buy a house in my hometown when I can afford it, and quite likely, I will travel back and forth between my hometown and the city that I’m living in now. That’s because even though my hometown is beautiful, it doesn’t have the kind of jobs that I am interested in.

### Where would you like to live?
Ideally, I would be able to afford an apartment in the city that I’m working in now, and a town house in my hometown. I’ll live in the city during the weekdays and travel back to my hometown to spend the weekends and holidays.

### Did you learn about the history of your hometown at school?
Actually, not really. In history class, the teacher only focused on textbooks. We were asked to memorize the historical events of the world and of our country, but nobody taught us about our hometown. Perhaps it’s too small to be mentioned.
实际上，并非如此。在历史课上，老师只关注课本内容。我们被要求记住世界和本国的历史事件，但没有人给我们讲解我们家乡的相关情况。也许是因为它太小了，所以没有被提及。

### Did you learn about the culture of your hometown in your childhood?
of course， when i was a child, my family often taught me the cultrure of our hometown, For instance,During the Spring Festival, we had special customs such as making and eating dumplings together as a family. ==**It was more than just a meal**==; it symbolized unity and the hope for a good year ahead. These experiences in my childhood ==have deeply engraved== the rich culture of my hometown ==in== my heart.

### Please describe your hometown a little.
Well, it’s a small town where the weather is mild and the seasons is distinct. There’s no major public transport facilities like a train station or airport, however, the roads are solid and lead to all directions. Traveling is easy if you have a vehicle(car). Most importantly, people there are very nice and modest.
是一个小小的城镇，四季分明，气候温和，没有主要的
