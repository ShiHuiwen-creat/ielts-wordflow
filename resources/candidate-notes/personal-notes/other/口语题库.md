## 题型
### 1. 喜好类题型

- **题目特点**：询问考生对某事物的喜好，如 “Do you like reading?” “Do you enjoy traveling?” 等。
- **答题思路**：先表明喜好与否，阐述原因，可适当举例。
- **万能表达**：
    - **表明喜好**：Yes, I do. I'm really into... / I'm quite fond of... / I enjoy... a lot.
    - **不喜欢**：No, I don't. I'm not really into... / I'm not fond of...
    - **阐述原因**：because it gives me a sense of...（如 pleasure, relaxation 等）/ It's...（如 interesting, exciting 等） / It allows me to...（如 meet new people, learn new things 等）
    - **举例**：For example, last week I...（描述与喜好相关的具体经历）

### 2. 习惯类题型

- **题目特点**：关于考生日常行为习惯，例如 “Do you often watch movies?” “How often do you exercise?”
- **答题思路**：回答频率，解释为何有此频率，可结合具体事例。
- **万能表达**：
    - **表达频率**：I...（如 watch movies）quite often /regularly./ I...（如 exercise）once or twice a week. / I don't...（如 go shopping）very often, maybe once a month.
    - **解释原因**：I do it so often because...（说明频繁做的原因）/ I don't do it frequently because...（解释不常做的原因）
    - **结合事例**：Last time I...（描述最近一次相关经历）

### 3. 经历类题型

- **题目特点**：考查考生过往经历，像 “Have you ever been to a concert?” “Did you have a memorable trip?”
- **答题思路**：回答是否有此经历，若有则简述经历，包括时间、地点、事件等；若没有可说明原因。
- **万能表达**：
    - **有经历**：Yes, I have. I remember it was...（时间）when I...（描述经历）at...（地点）. It was really...（如 amazing, unforgettable 等）.  
        **有经历** ：是的，我有。我记得那是......（时间）当我...（描述经历）at...（地点）.这真的是......（如 amazing， unforgetable 等）。
    - **无经历**：No, I haven't. The main reason is that I've been really busy, so I haven't had the chance yet.

### 4. 观点类题型

- **题目特点**：询问考生对某事物的看法，比如 “What do you think of online learning?” “How do you feel about fast food?”
- **答题思路**：表明观点，给出理由，可适当对比。
- **万能表达**：
    - **表明观点**：I think it's...（如 a good idea, quite convenient 等） / In my opinion, it has both advantages and disadvantages.
    - **阐述理由**：One reason is that... / Another reason is...
    - **对比**：Compared with...（传统方式等）, it's...（如 more efficient, less time - consuming 等）  
        **对比** ：与...（传统方式等），它是......（如 更高效，更省时 - 耗时等）
    - It depends on the context，In some cases，a can。。。On the other hand，b can 。。。
    - So a balanced amount works best for me.（一个平衡的数量是最适合我的）

### 5. 未来计划类题型

- **题目特点**：关于考生未来打算，如 “Do you plan to learn a new language?” “What are your plans for the coming holiday?”
- **答题思路**：表明是否有计划，有则说明计划内容，无则解释原因。
- **万能表达**：
    - **有计划**：Yes, I do. I plan to...（详细说明计划）. I think it will be...（如 interesting, beneficial 等）.
    - **无计划**：No, I don't have any specific plans at the moment. Maybe because I'm still not sure what I really want to do.
