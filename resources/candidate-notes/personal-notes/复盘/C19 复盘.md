1. **intestines** /ɪnˈtɛstɪnz/
    
    - 名词，复数形式（单数：intestine）
    - 指**肠道**，尤其是小肠和大肠，是消化系统的重要部分。
    - 例：_The small intestines absorb most of the nutrients from food._
    - 引申义：有时用于比喻“内在部分”或“勇气”（如 _have the guts/intestines to do something_，但更常用 _guts_）。
2. **former** /ˈfɔːrmər/
    
    - 形容词，意为**以前的，前任的**；常与 _latter_（后者）配对使用。
    - 例：_She worked as a teacher and a writer; the former job was more stable._
    - 注意：首字母大写的 _Former_ 有时在正式文体中指“前……”（如 _Former President_）。
3. **tension** /ˈtɛnʃn/
    
    - 名词，有多重含义：
        
        - **物理张力**（如绳子、肌肉的拉力）
        - **紧张局势/关系**（外交、人际）
        - **心理压力/焦虑**
        
    - 例：_There was tension between the two departments._  
        _Muscle tension can cause headaches._
4. **court** /kɔːrt/
    
    - 名词：
        
        - **法院**（judicial setting）
        - **球场**（如 tennis court）
        - **宫廷**（royal court）
        
    - 动词：**追求；招致（危险、批评等）**
        - _He is courting controversy with his statements._
        - _They courted each other for two years before marrying._
5. **nutrition** /nuˈtrɪʃn/
    
    - 名词，指**营养；营养学**，强调食物对健康和生长的作用。
    - 常见搭配：_good/poor nutrition_, _nutritional value_, _balanced nutrition_
    - 例：_Proper nutrition is essential for children’s development._
6. **metal** /ˈmɛtəl/
    
    - 名词：
        
        - **金属**（如 iron, copper, aluminum）；具有导电性、延展性等。
        - （非正式）**勇气，毅力**（= _mettle_ 的误用但常见；标准用法应为 _mettle_，但 _metal_ 在口语中有时混用）
        
    - 形容词（常作复合词）：_metal detector_, _metal band_
    - 注意：_heavy metal_ 可指重金属音乐或有毒重金属（如 lead, mercury），需依语境判断。

使用生词组成的句子：
The **former** athlete’s sudden abdominal pain — later diagnosed as inflammation of the **intestines** — **was attributed to** poor **nutrition** combined with excessive physical **tension** during training; notably, a routine scan at the sports medicine **court** (clinic) also revealed trace levels of toxic **metal** accumulation, possibly from contaminated supplements.

 A. The state estimated that very few people were involved in piracy.
 B. Attitudes towards piracy changed shortly after the Iliad and the Odyssey were written.
 C. Important officials were known to occasionally take part in piracy.
 D. Every citizen regarded pirate attacks on cities as unacceptable.
 E. A favourable view of piracy is evident in certain ancient Greek texts.

答案句：
_Even high-ranking members of the state were not beyond engaging in such activities… Athenian ambassadors… captured a ship… taking the wealth…_

16 a mention of the circumstances in which states in the ancient world would make use of pirates
提及古代世界各国在何种情况下会利用海盗

- **答案段落：C**
    
- **原文原句：**
    
    > "One should also add that it was not unknown in the first and second millennia BCE for **governments to resort to pirates' services, especially during wartime**, employing their skills and numbers against their opponents."
    
- **解析：** 题目询问国家利用海盗的 **"circumstances"**（情况/环境）。原文明确指出政府会使用海盗服务（resort to pirates' services），并给出了具体的情况：**"especially during wartime"**（特别是在战时）。



### **18. an explanation of how some people were encouraged not to return to piracy**

_(解释了如何鼓励一些人不再重操海盗旧业)_

- **答案段落：G**
    
- **原文原句：**
    
    > "...many more were **offered land in fertile areas located far from the sea**. Instead of a maritime menace, Rome got productive farmers that further boosted its economy."
    
- **解析：** 题目问的是如何 **"encouraged not to return to piracy"**（鼓励不再做海盗）。原文解释了庞培的策略：给海盗们提供 **"land... far from the sea"**（远离海洋的土地），让他们变成农民。远离海洋且有了生计，这就是防止他们重操旧业的方法。
